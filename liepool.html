About Liverpool FC

Liverpool Football Club is one of the biggest football clubs in the world. Founded in 1892, the club has won numerous league titles, Champions League trophies, and domestic cups. Liverpool is famous for its passionate fans, legendary players, and iconic stadium — Anfield.
